# PROMIS Requirements: Open Questions & Pending Decisions Register

## Overview

This register tracks all unresolved business decisions, pending University master data, and open governance questions. In accordance with the golden rule:
> **Do not invent missing University rules, approval authorities, organizational structures, or statutory procedures.**

Items in this register are actively tracked and must be resolved by authorized USTED stakeholders prior to final system deployment.

---

## 1. Quarterly Procurement Plan Review & Revision (FR-049, FR-050, FR-051)

While the client has officially confirmed that procurement plans undergo quarterly reviews and revisions, the detailed operational procedures and version association details remain `TO BE CONFIRMED`. Note that Questions 7 and 8 relate directly to **FR-051 (Plan Version Association)**, where each plan-linked requisition retains a reference to the specific plan version under which it was submitted. The following 14 questions require authoritative institutional decisions:

1. **What exactly triggers the quarterly review?** (e.g. A fixed calendar date, a formal notification issued by the Directorate of Procurement, or the start of a fiscal quarter?)
2. **Who initiates the quarterly review?** (e.g. Central Procurement, Finance, or the Planning Officer within each planning entity?)
3. **Who is allowed to revise an approved plan?** (e.g. Is it restricted to the original Planning Officer, the Head of Department, or a designated planning committee?)
4. **Who approves the revised plan?** (e.g. Does it require the same hierarchy as the original annual plan, or an expedited approval level?)
5. **Is every quarterly review required to produce a revision, or only when changes are needed?** (e.g. Can an entity certify *"No revision required for Q2"*?)
6. **Can a plan be revised outside the quarterly review period?** (e.g. Are emergency or ad-hoc revisions permitted between quarters?)
7. **What happens to requests already submitted against the previous plan version?** (e.g. Do in-flight requisitions continue under the old baseline, or are they re-evaluated?)
8. **What happens to approved requests if the related plan is subsequently revised?** (e.g. Are approved commitments grandfathered in, or must remaining quantities be recalculated?)
9. **Are increases and decreases in quantity allowed?** (e.g. Can an entity increase laptop quantities from 20 to 30, or only reallocate existing limits?)
10. **Can new items be introduced during a quarterly revision?** (e.g. Adding an item not contemplated in the original annual procurement plan?)
11. **Can items be removed?** (e.g. De-committing an item that will no longer be procured during the fiscal year?)
12. **Does a revision require Budget revalidation?** (e.g. If quantities or costs increase, must Finance re-authorize the revised ceiling?)
13. **Does a revised plan require Procurement approval again?** (e.g. Does the Directorate of Procurement conduct an institutional check before the revision takes effect?)
14. **How is the effective version of the plan determined?** (e.g. Is Version 2.0 active immediately upon Director approval, or on a specific effective date?)
